# Review: Novena for Passing an Exam

Doctrine, lengths, digits and words, titles, capitals and the Day 9 step are all sound. Every field is within the skill's word and character limits. All 10 icons exist in `tools/icons/`. Nothing promises a pass. Kindness to those who fail is strong (Day 9, tip). No superstition: Day 7 rules out "lucky charms", and Day 4 says the Cupertino story "is not a promise". Confirmed: Joseph of Cupertino's dates (1603–1663), feast (Sep 18), Copertino, his struggles with learning, priesthood, ecstasies and canonization in 1767 (Clement XIII), and the diaconate story, which is tradition and is told as such. Also confirmed: Aquinas's dates, feast (Jan 28), the Summa, the Corpus Christi hymns, Doctor of the Church, Leo XIII's 1880 brief naming him patron of Catholic schools, early biographers saying he always prayed before study or writing (William of Tocco), the St. John and the bow story (ST II-II q.168 a.2), and the "straw" episode of December 1273. "Seat of Wisdom" is in the Litany of Loreto. Scripture spot-check: James 1:5 matches BibleGateway's RSVCE word for word. Psalm 127:2 and Colossians 3:23–24 could not be fetched because the session's WebFetch budget ran out (see the end of this file). Every passage fits how its reflection uses it. Colossians 3:23–24 is addressed to slaves (3:22), but applying it to any honest work is a standard reading.

1. **MUST: days[1].more, sentence 2 (authorship of the prayer before study).** "He composed a prayer before study that students still use" states his authorship as fact. The *Ante studium* ("Creator ineffabilis") is traditionally attributed to Thomas, but it is not among his securely authentic works, and the skill allows only facts we would stake our name on. Replace with (73 words in total): "A prayer before study long attributed to him, still used by students, asks the Creator of all things to guide the beginning of their work, direct its progress and bring it to completion." In sources.md, change "His prayer before study ('Creator of all things') is paraphrased" to "The prayer before study traditionally attributed to him ('Creator of all things') is paraphrased; its authorship is not certain."

2. **SHOULD: tips[1].p ("start of a new school year").** "You can also pray this novena at the start of a new school year." does not fit the text: every day prays about "the exam", Day 1's step asks for the exam's name and date, and Day 7 is exam day. Cut the sentence. A school-year novena is already listed as a separate idea in PROJECT_LOG, and Wisdom and Understanding covers learning in general.

3. **SHOULD: tips[1].p (when it gets too heavy).** The tip offers no prudent step for the student whose anxiety, or whose grief over a failed result, becomes overwhelming. Board-exam results can hit very hard. Full replacement, which also covers item 2: "If you can, go to Confession and attend Mass during these 9 days, and receive Communion before the exam. Pair prayer with good preparation: a study plan (Day&nbsp;4), enough sleep (Day&nbsp;5), and no leaked questions, copied answers or fixers (Day&nbsp;6). If the result is not what you hoped, be gentle with yourself and others; many pass on a retake. If worry or disappointment becomes too heavy to carry, talk to someone you trust: a parent, a priest or a counselor. At “N.”, say the student’s name; if you are praying for yourself, say “me”."

4. **SHOULD: days[2].refl, last sentence (wisdom vs. memory).** "Today we ask boldly for that gift: understanding as we study, a memory that keeps what we have learned, and good judgment…" presents memory and study skills as part of "that gift", meaning wisdom. Wisdom and understanding are gifts of the Holy Spirit (CCC 1831; Isaiah 11:2), but a good memory is a natural help, not one of those gifts. Replace with (84 words in total): "Today we ask boldly for that gift, and for the help we need now: understanding as we study, a memory that keeps what we have learned, and good judgment to use it well, now and in the work ahead."

5. **SHOULD: days[2].more, sentence 3 (Luke 2:19).** "Mary kept the events of His life and pondered them in her heart (Luke 2:19)" goes beyond the text: 2:19 is about the shepherds' visit at His birth, and 2:51 is about finding Him in the Temple. Replace with "Mary kept the events of His childhood and pondered them in her heart (Luke 2:19, 51)." (74 words in total, unchanged)

6. **SHOULD: days[8].prayer (who "Lord" is).** The prayer opens "Lord", then thanks the saints "for carrying our prayers before Jesus", and returns to "Your loving will". If "Lord" means Jesus, the prayer speaks of Him in the third person partway through. Open with "Heavenly Father, on this ninth day…", as the Wisdom and Understanding novena does (79 words). The healing novena has the same pattern.

   - days[0].more: "he struggled with his studies and was slow to learn" repeats "slow" from the first sentence. Use "he struggled with his studies and found learning hard."
   - `status` is already "published". The skill says "draft" until the user approves; make sure that approval was given before images and credits are final.
