# Review: Novena for the Healing of the Sick (draft)

Doctrine, lengths, spelling and capitals are all sound. Confirmed: Tobit 12:15; Camillus's dates, conversion, the 1598 flood (Subleyras painting, Museo di Roma), the "only sick child" line (his Rule) and patronages; Bernadette's dates, cholera and asthma, "my business is to be ill"; Our Lady's "in the next".

1. **MUST: saints[1].bio.** "troubled him all his life" is wrong: the sore began while he was a soldier, and Butler gives "forty-six years". Replace with (34 words): "Soldier and gambler, converted in 1575, who gave his life to the sick. Though a leg wound troubled him for the rest of his life, he founded the Camillians, the Ministers of the Sick."

2. **MUST: days[3].more, sentence 1.** Our Lady did not ask Bernadette "to dig". On 25 February 1858 she told her to "go and drink at the spring and wash" there, and Bernadette scraped the mud until water came (lourdes-france.com, Ninth Apparition). The replacement is in item 5.

3. **MUST: days[0].more.** "At the tomb… Jesus Himself wept" is inaccurate: He weeps in John 11:35 and reaches the tomb only in 11:38. Replace with: "Grieving for His friend Lazarus, Jesus Himself wept (John 11:35)."

4. **SHOULD: tips[1].p and days[7].step (Anointing).** Many families delay, thinking it is "last rites". The Church gives it to anyone in danger from sickness or old age, and before serious surgery (CCC 1514–1515).
   - tips[1].p: "If you can, go to Confession and attend Mass during these nine days. Ask a priest about the Anointing of the Sick, which is for anyone seriously ill or facing major surgery, not only the dying. Pair prayer with good care: follow medical advice, offer practical help (Day&nbsp;6), and be wary of anyone who promises a sure cure. At “N.”, say the sick person’s name; if you are praying for yourself, say “me”."
   - days[7].step (30 words): "If you can, go to Confession this week, and gently ask N. or their family whether they would like a priest to bring Communion or the Anointing of the Sick."

5. **SHOULD: days[3].more (not being cured is not weak faith).** Nothing says this outright. CCC 1508: "even the most intense prayers do not always obtain the healing of all illnesses" (2 Cor 12:9). Replace the paragraph with this one, which also fixes item 2 (75 words): "At Lourdes in 1858, Our Lady told St. Bernadette to drink at the spring and wash there; she scraped the muddy ground and a spring began to flow. Some pilgrims have been cured there; many more have gone home with peace. If healing does not come, it is never a sign of weak faith: St. Paul begged three times to be freed of his affliction, and was answered with grace (2 Corinthians 12:8–9)."

6. **SHOULD: days[2].more (hope of eternal life).** Christ's victory over death is never mentioned, though the family may lose their friend (CCC 1505). Replace the last two sentences with: "For now, it is enough to trust that Christ is with the sick in their pain and with all who love them. Even the last valley is not walked alone: Christ has passed through death and opened the way to eternal life." (73 words in total)

7. **SHOULD: days[8].more.** The Bernadette line arrives with nothing leading into it, and on the last day it can sound like "don't hope for a cure". Raphael is also featured on only two days, where the skill asks for about three. Replace "These are signs… but in the next." with: "Before St. Raphael told Tobit and Tobias who he was, he urged them to praise God and give thanks for all He had done (Tobit 12:6)." (73 words in total)

8. **SHOULD: tips[1].p.** There is no instruction for a sick person praying for themselves. There is also no warning against paid "sure cures" or healers who tell people to stop treatment, to match the debt novena's "5-6" warning (CCC 2117). The tip in item 4 covers both.

9. **SHOULD: days[6].more.**
   - "and for the Church" is not attested. Bernadette offered her sufferings "for the big sinner" (Aleteia, 2024).
   - "who lived with Parkinson’s disease, taught… (1984)" suggests his illness was behind the teaching, but the Parkinson's came years later.
   - Replace from "and she offered" to the end with: "and she offered her sufferings for sinners. On the feast of Our Lady of Lourdes in 1984, Pope St. John Paul II taught that suffering joined to Christ shares in His work of redemption (Salvifici Doloris). We may pray for healing and offer our pain at the same time." (74 words in total)

10. **SHOULD: days[4].more.** "That is why St. Raphael has long been invoked…" gives a single cause; his name and Tobit 12:14 also explain the patronage. Change it to "This is one reason St. Raphael…".

11. **SHOULD: minor.**
    - days[1].step: "Send N. or their family a short message, call or visit today, so they know N. is being prayed for." (works if N. is too ill).
    - days[3].step: add "…or have a Mass offered for N."
    - sources.md: add Tobit 12:15 (saints[0].bio), 2 Cor 12:8–9 and Tobit 12:6.
