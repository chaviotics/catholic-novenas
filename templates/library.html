How a novena works

  • Nine days, one intention

    A novena is prayer for one intention over nine days, after the Apostles and Mary, who prayed together in the days before Pentecost (Acts 1:14).

  • About ten minutes a day

    Each day has an opening prayer, a Scripture passage and reflection, the day’s prayer, and the closing prayers.

  • Together or alone

    One person leads and everyone responds. Your progress, start date and intention stay only on your phone.
