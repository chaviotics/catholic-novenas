How a novena works

  • 9 days, 1 intention

    A novena is 9 days of prayer for a single intention, after the Apostles and Mary, who prayed together before Pentecost (Acts 1:14).

  • About 10 minutes a day

    Each day has an opening prayer, a Scripture passage and reflection, the day’s prayer, and the closing prayers.

  • Stays on your device

    Your progress, start date and intention are saved only on your phone or computer, never uploaded.
